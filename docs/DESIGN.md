# My PhD OS: final UI design ("Editorial, calmer")

Status: binding. Winner: Direction C (Editorial) with grafts from A and B and every judge "mustFix" resolved.
The mockups the section 8 headings refer to (`deadlines|calendar|habits|timeline|personal|settings|dialog|palette`, light and dark, 1440x775 CSS px) were design-time artefacts and are not kept in the repository; the running app is the reference.

## 1. Principles (the owner's terms)

The complaint: too flashy, not 简约大气. Reading: 大气 is a by-product of 克制 (restraint), 减法 (subtraction), 留白 (whitespace), 低饱和 (little colour). The result must look like a well-set printed page, not a dashboard.

| #   | Principle                   | Rule an engineer can check                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Subtract first              | Anything that repeats information, decorates, or announces the normal state is deleted (second page title, KPI tiles, chips, legends, seconds clock, brand tile, dashed boxes, key caps, row stripes). |
| 2   | Colour is the exception     | Neutrals plus ONE signal colour. Max one coloured numeral per screen; coloured status marks are small (glyph plus word, 13-15 px).                                                                     |
| 3   | Structure is felt, not seen | Order of separation: whitespace, then a 1 px hairline, then (floating layers only) a ring + shadow. No cards, no boxes, no zebra, no nested containers.                                                |
| 4   | Type does the hierarchy     | Six sizes, two weights, three ink tiers. One 64 px numeral per app, one 40 px title per page.                                                                                                          |
| 5   | One ink primary per view    | Near-black button (near-white in dark), top right, in the 48 px strip. Everything else is a text link or ghost.                                                                                        |
| 6   | Never faint                 | Text 4.5:1 minimum (7:1 for primary text), control edges and focus 3:1, hairlines are decoration only.                                                                                                 |
| 7   | Nothing ambient             | No pulse, no ticking chrome (minute-precision dateline), no hover fills that look like buttons, motion 100-150 ms.                                                                                     |
| 8   | Both themes authored        | Dark is its own palette (paper `#0F0E0D`-ish, floating layers one step lighter), not an inversion.                                                                                                     |
| 9   | Biolinum, not a substitute  | Linux Biolinum bundled; hierarchy from size, ink tier and spacing; Bold is rationed (section 4.3).                                                                                                     |

Judge fixes folded in: warm paper instead of stark white; more air above the title; collapsed Timeline checks; one coloured numeral per screen; "Urgent" word plus glyph wherever red appears; Gantt clip cues and 3:1 bar outlines; small caps limited to two roles; lighter habit grid; Original (UTC-12) moved off every non-lead row (still on hover/focus/peek and in the DOM).

## 2. Colour policy

### 2.1 Where chroma may appear (and nowhere else)

Only `--signal` (vermilion, light `oklch(.51 .19 33)`, dark `oklch(.72 .17 36)`) carries chroma. Neutrals use hue 85, chroma at most 0.004.

| Place                                                                   | Allowed form                               | Notes                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lead conference countdown (Deadlines, under 24 h)                       | 64 px numerals + units + seconds           | The ONLY large coloured thing in the app. Always paired with `Urgent` glyph + word under it.                            |
| Status words Urgent, Overdue, Missed, Failed                            | 13-15 px text + 14-16 px glyph             | Always word + glyph; never a fill, chip or tint.                                                                        |
| "Past target by N days" (Gantt note) and the error text of a form field | 13 px text + glyph                         | One per row.                                                                                                            |
| Count text `(1 overdue)` in the Personal lead sentence                  | none: rendered in ink                      | Do not colour (judges: red 17h/2d numerals broke calm).                                                                 |
| Destructive confirm button                                              | solid `--signal` fill, `--signal-ink` text | Only inside an `alertdialog`.                                                                                           |
| Everything else                                                         | forbidden                                  | Buttons, tabs, links, focus ring, selected rows, bars, grids, calendar events, badges, banners, tints, gradients, glow. |

Rule "one coloured numeral per screen": Calendar panel and Personal list countdowns are ink; their urgency is carried by the signal glyph + word beside them.

### 3.3 Usage rules

1. Text colour is one of ink, ink-2, ink-3, signal. Never ink-4 for anything the user must read.
2. Never paint a stored per-record hex directly; route it through `oklch(var(--user-l) var(--user-c) h)` (relative colour syntax, Chromium 119+; the app runs 152).
3. Palette colours are read through the raw property (`var(--status-urgent)`), never `var(--color-status-urgent)` (test-enforced).
4. A tint (hover, selected, track) is always an ink alpha over paper; no hued tint exists.

## 4. Typography: Linux Biolinum

### 4.1 Scale (six sizes; nothing else may appear)

| Role    | Size / line | Tracking | Weight | Ink                           | Use                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------- | ----------- | -------- | ------ | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| meta    | 13 / 18     | 0        | 400    | ink-3 (ink-2 for helper text) | datelines, axis labels, captions, units, rank lines, hints. Floor: nothing below 13 px (12 px only for the all-small-caps label rendering at 15 px size, see 4.4). |
| body    | 15 / 22     | 0        | 400    | ink / ink-2                   | body, controls, nav, buttons, dates in rows, tabs                                                                                                                  |
| lead    | 18 / 26     | 0        | 400    | ink                           | row titles, panel section headings (ink-2), palette input, lead sentences                                                                                          |
| title   | 28 / 34     | -0.012em | 400    | ink                           | lead-row title, row countdown numerals, dialog titles, calendar day numerals                                                                                       |
| display | 40 / 44     | -0.012em | 400    | ink                           | page title (one per page), range title, streak numerals                                                                                                            |
| hero    | 64 / 64     | -0.012em | 400    | signal when under 24 h        | the single lead countdown                                                                                                                                          |

Biolinum's x-height is 0.46 em, so 15 px is about SF 13 px. Negative tracking only at 28 px and above. Never letter-space body text.

### 4.2 Numerals and units

- Numerals are tabular lining by default; still set `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` on `body`. No monospace anywhere (the Settings DB path also uses Biolinum with tabular numerals).
- Countdown anatomy: numerals at the row's size, units (`d h m s`) at 13 px ink-3 (signal when the countdown is urgent), seconds at 13 px (18 px on the hero). Hours and minutes are zero-padded to two digits, days are not ("9 d 09 h", "09 h 29 m") so the layout does not jump when crossing 10 h or 10 d.
- Dateline in the strip: `Thursday, Nov 26, 2026 . 18:48 PST` minute precision, no seconds. Seconds exist only inside countdowns under 24 h (spec 18).

### 4.3 The two weights

| Weight | Allowed only for                                                                                                           |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 700    | (1) the next event on the Calendar (grid mark title and Today row), (2) the one habit still to do today, (3) nothing else. |
| 400    | everything else, including titles and numerals.                                                                            |

Tailwind remap in the theme block: `--font-weight-medium: 400; --font-weight-semibold: 700; --font-weight-bold: 700`. Guard test: grep `src/renderer` for `font-bold|font-semibold|font-weight: (6|7)` and fail unless the file is on the allow-list (`CalendarBoard`, `TodayPanel`, `HabitCard`). Set `font-synthesis: none` (no fake bold or bold italic).

### 4.4 Italic and small caps

| Style                                                             | Only for                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Italic (400i)                                                     | states of absence: `Waiting for a date`, `TBD` hints, `Passed N days ago`. Not for the wordmark (deleted), not for emphasis.                                                                                                                               |
| `font-variant-caps: all-small-caps`, 15 px, tracking .05em, ink-3 | exactly two roles: (a) the category label in the Gantt/Settings label rail, (b) the rule tag of a Timeline check (`PAST TARGET`, `OVERLAP`). `text-transform: uppercase` is forbidden (census `uppercaseTextEls` = 0). Max 8 small-caps elements per page. |

"Updated", "Deadline changed", "Followed" are plain 13 px words, not caps.

### 4.5 Glyphs, fallback and truncation

- Stack: `"Linux Biolinum", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei", "Noto Sans", sans-serif, "Apple Color Emoji", "Segoe UI Emoji"`. Biolinum has no CJK, no ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ ↵ ✓ ✕ ⚠; those fall back (keyboard hints "⌘K" use the system glyph; all icons are SVG). Add `text-autospace: normal` and `:lang(zh) { letter-spacing: 0 }`.
- Biolinum is wider than SF: every fixed width in the mocks was re-tuned (sidebar 216, panel 288, deadline row columns 440 / flexible / 168, rail 304). Re-check any new fixed width with the real titles.
- Truncation: single-line titles use `text-overflow: ellipsis` with a `title` tooltip; row titles in lists may wrap to two lines at most (no ellipsis for a title shorter than 60 chars at 1440 px). Calendar marks wrap up to three lines then clip. Countdown, date and status never truncate (they own their column).

### 4.6 Bundling

Linux Biolinum is bundled (three faces, OFL 1.1, no CDN); see `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` Decision 68.
